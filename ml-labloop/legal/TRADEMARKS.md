# LabLoop Trademark Policy

`LabLoop` is a trademark or potential trademark of Alexander Bikeyev.

Cloudcell Limited may use and administer the `LabLoop` mark with the trademark
owner's permission.

This policy explains how the `LabLoop` name and any official `LabLoop` logos,
icons, slogans, and visual identity may be used by the community, contributors,
users, consultants, educators, and commercial partners.

This policy applies separately from the software license. The ML-LabLoop
software may be available under the Apache License 2.0, but that license does not
grant trademark rights.

## Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to:

1. Protect users from confusion about the source, quality, and authenticity of
   ML-LabLoop software and services.
2. Allow fair community use of the LabLoop name.
3. Prevent misleading forks, rebranded distributions, or competing services from
   appearing to be official.
4. Preserve the reputation and identity of the ML-LabLoop project.

## Protected Mark

The protected mark is:

- `LabLoop`

This policy also applies to any official ML-LabLoop logo, icon, product badge,
splash screen, visual identity, or confusingly similar mark used in connection
with ML experimentation labs, sandboxed execution environments, VM templates,
or related software.

For clarity, this policy does not claim `ml-scientist`, `labloop`, or other
adjacent project names as protected trademarks under this policy unless and
until Alexander Bikeyev separately publishes a policy or registration covering
those marks.

## Permitted Uses

You may use the LabLoop mark without prior written permission in the following
cases, provided that your use is truthful, non-misleading, and does not imply
endorsement by Alexander Bikeyev or Cloudcell Limited.

### Referring to the Project

You may refer to ML-LabLoop by name when discussing, reviewing, teaching, or
describing the project.

Examples:

- "This tutorial explains how to use ML-LabLoop."
- "Our research group runs its experiments in a LabLoop lab."
- "This integration targets ML-LabLoop."

### Community Discussion

You may use the LabLoop name in blog posts, videos, documentation,
presentations, academic work, issue reports, and community discussion.

Examples:

- "ML-LabLoop installation guide"
- "Notes on the LabLoop security model"
- "LabLoop template customization"

### Compatibility Statements

You may use the LabLoop name to describe compatibility.

Examples:

- "Compatible with ML-LabLoop"
- "Plugin for LabLoop"
- "Template for LabLoop labs"

You must not imply that your plugin, template, integration, service, or product
is official unless Alexander Bikeyev or Cloudcell Limited has approved it in
writing.

### Forks

You may state that your project is a fork of ML-LabLoop.

Example:

- "This project is a fork of ML-LabLoop."

However, the fork must use a different name and branding unless Cloudcell
Limited gives written permission.

## Restricted Uses

You may not use the LabLoop mark in a way that suggests your product, service,
fork, company, website, package, or distribution is official, endorsed,
sponsored, or maintained by Alexander Bikeyev or Cloudcell Limited unless you
have written permission.

The following uses require prior written permission from Alexander Bikeyev or
Cloudcell Limited.

### Rebranding or White-Labeling

You may not rebrand ML-LabLoop, or any confusingly similar version of the
software, as your own official product.

Examples of prohibited use:

- Selling a modified version as "LabLoop Pro" without permission.
- Releasing a fork called "LabLoop Cloud" without permission.
- Releasing a fork called "LabLoop Enterprise" without permission.
- Replacing the logo and presenting the software as your own official LabLoop
  platform.

### Hosted or Managed Services

You may not use the LabLoop mark for a hosted, managed, SaaS, or multi-tenant
service unless you have written permission from Alexander Bikeyev or Cloudcell
Limited.

Examples of prohibited use:

- "LabLoop Cloud"
- "LabLoop Hosted"
- "Managed LabLoop Service"
- "LabLoop-as-a-Service"

This restriction is separate from the software license. The Apache License 2.0 grants
copyright permissions to run, modify, and distribute the software, but it does
not grant permission to use Alexander Bikeyev's trademarks, product names,
logos, or brand identity.

### Competing Products

You may not use the LabLoop mark, or confusingly similar marks, for a competing
commercial ML experimentation lab, sandbox platform, or VM-template product.

Examples of prohibited use:

- "LabLoop Enterprise" for an independently operated competing product.
- "LabLoop Labs" for a competing commercial platform.
- A fork branded so similarly that users may believe it is the official
  ML-LabLoop project.

### Domain Names and Social Media Accounts

You may not register or use domain names, social media handles, app names,
package names, or organization names that imply official status.

Examples requiring permission:

- `labloop.cloud`
- `lablooppro.com`
- `labloop-platform.com`
- `github.com/labloop-enterprise`
- `@labloopcloud`

Community names are acceptable if they clearly avoid confusion.

Examples that may be acceptable:

- `labloop-notes`
- `awesome-labloop`
- `labloop-tutorials`
- `labloop-plugin-examples`

### Logos and Visual Identity

You may not use the ML-LabLoop logo, icon, splash screen, or visual identity as
the primary logo for your own project, product, company, fork, website, SaaS
platform, consulting service, or commercial offering.

You may use the official ML-LabLoop logo or icon in educational or descriptive
materials, provided that:

1. The use is accurate and non-misleading.
2. The logo or icon is not modified in a way that damages the brand.
3. The logo or icon is not used more prominently than your own name or
   branding.
4. The use does not imply endorsement or partnership.

## Naming Rules for Forks and Derivative Projects

If you publish a fork or derivative version of ML-LabLoop, you must use a
distinct name.

Acceptable examples:

- "LabForge, a fork of ML-LabLoop"
- "SandboxGrid, based on ML-LabLoop"
- "Acme Experiment Lab, derived from ML-LabLoop"

Unacceptable examples without permission:

- "LabLoop Plus"
- "LabLoop Enterprise"
- "LabLoop Cloud"
- "LabLoop Pro"
- "LabLoop for Business"

Forks and derivative projects must also remove or clearly distinguish official
ML-LabLoop logos, splash screens, application icons, and product identity
unless otherwise permitted by Alexander Bikeyev or Cloudcell Limited.
